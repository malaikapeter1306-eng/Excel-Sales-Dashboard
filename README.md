# Excel-Sales-Dashboard
This project focuses on analyzing sales performance using Microsoft Excel. The analysis uses different data sets to identify sales patterns and provide a summarized view of business performance through an interactive dashboard.  

**Analysis Objective**
Sales performance across different categories and segments
Customer and shipping patterns
Product and order performance
Return activity
Key trends and business insights

**Skills Used**
* Data cleaning and organization
* Pivot Tables
* Pivot Charts
* Dashboard creation
* Data summarization
* Filtering and analysis
* Business-oriented data visualization

**Dashboard**
The final dashboard provides a visual summary of the sales analysis and allows users to review key business information in a consolidated format.

**Key Insights & Business Recommendations**
1) California generates the highest total profit among the states in the dataset.
   * Investigate what is driving California's profitability, particularly its order volume, product mix, customer segments, and discount levels, and consider using those patterns as a benchmark when evaluating lower-performing states.
2) The South region generates substantial sales compared with the other regions, but its profitability remains significantly lower. This is largely driven by North Carolina, which also records high sales but comparatively low profitability. This indicates that higher sales volume in the South region is not necessarily translating into higher profits
   * Factors such as product mix, discounting, shipping costs, and order-level margins need to be investagated on a root level to find out the reasoing for difrences in Profits and Sales.
   * Prioritize higher-margin products and introduce minimum-margin thresholds for discounted or large orders.
3) In Sub-Catrgory Tables generates substantial sales but has negative overall profit.
   * Reassess pricing and discount levels for Tables.
   * shift customers toward profitable furniture alternatives rather than pursuing additional volume through discounts.
4) The Top 3 Least profitiable products are responsible for more than $51K in losses.
   * Flag these products for immediate profitability review and consider repricing, reducing discounts, renegotiating procurement costs, or discontinuing consistently loss-making products if their margins cannot be improved.
